contrast-color()

A native CSS function that returns black or white — whichever has higher contrast against the given color. No JavaScript, no Sass mixin, no build-time computation.

contrast-color(<color>) → black | white
contrast-color(#7c3aed) → white
contrast-color(#fbbf24) → black

Limitation

The Mid-Tone Problem

contrast-color() always picks the better of black or white — but in the L 40–60% zone, the winning option is often only 5–6:1. The function is working correctly; the background color is the constraint.

Mid-tone zone: the best available contrast (what contrast-color() picks) is below 7:1 (WCAG AAA). The function is correct — black or white is the right answer — but the background color itself is the limiting factor. Consider choosing a lighter or darker hue to get out of this range.
